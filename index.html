Do I need a referral to book an exam?

No — you can book a comprehensive eye exam directly with us, no referral required. Specialist procedures may need a prior consultation.

How long does a first visit take?

Plan for about 60–75 minutes for a new-patient exam, including imaging and time to discuss results with your doctor.

Is LASIK covered by insurance?

Most insurance treats LASIK as elective, but we offer transparent self-pay pricing and financing plans — ask at your consultation.

Do you see children?

Yes, from age 3 onward. Pediatric exams are shorter and designed to be low-stress for younger patients.
